About Me

Hi y’all! I’m Eli, a sophomore working student currently balancing my job with my online BA in Multimedia Studies. As for my hobbies, I absolutely love singing, serving in my local youth ministry, and just enjoying some quality downtime with my family and friends whenever I get the chance.

I decided to take MMS 171 because typography is such a massive part of design that goes way beyond just picking a nice font. I really want to learn how to use text effectively to guide people's eyes and make digital layouts actually click and engage an audience. Going through this class, I'm genuinely excited to pick up new design tricks, get better at working with text in my projects, and get to know and learn alongside all of you this term!
